Hosting and server requests

This website is hosted by Vercel Inc., USA. When you open a page or download a file, your browser sends technical request data to Vercel's infrastructure: your IP address, the date and time, the requested address, the referring page, and your browser's user agent. This is necessary to deliver the website and to keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; legitimate interest in operating a working, secure website).

The website uses your browser's user agent to decide whether to show its mobile or desktop layout. This happens on each request and nothing is stored for it.

Vercel is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which covers the transfer of this data to the USA (Art. 45 GDPR).

The website itself sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking, and loads all fonts and images from its own server.

Contact, application and partnership forms

When you send one of the forms on this website, the data you enter (name, email address, the links you provide, the position you select and your message) is sent to a server function on Vercel and from there by email to the controller. It is used only to answer your request, to consider your application or to discuss a partnership (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request concerns steps you ask for, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in answering inquiries). Your email address is used as the reply address.

The website does not store form submissions. The email is sent through Resend (Resend, Inc., USA), which processes it on our behalf and keeps the email and its delivery logs for 30 days. Resend stores data in the USA; the transfer is covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

To keep out spam, the forms contain a hidden field and check how quickly they were sent; your IP address is used briefly in the server's memory to limit repeated submissions and is not stored.

Received form emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and are then deleted, unless legal retention duties apply.

Contact by email

If you write to the email address above, your email and the data in it are processed to answer you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where it concerns steps you ask for).

YouTube videos

The Tutorials and DIY pages contain YouTube videos. They are not loaded when you open the page: nothing is sent to YouTube until you click the play button. Only then is the video loaded from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. From that moment, YouTube receives at least your IP address and the page you are on, and may store or read information on your device and transfer data to Google LLC in the USA (covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework).

The legal basis is your consent by clicking play (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). You can avoid this by not starting the videos or by watching them directly on YouTube via the link shown. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Links to other websites

This website links to other websites, such as social networks (YouTube, Reddit, Instagram, Facebook, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok), chat groups (Discord, Signal, Telegram), GitHub, OSHWLab, Thingiverse, PlatformIO, product pages on AliExpress and the merchandise shop. No data is sent to them unless you follow a link; their own privacy policies apply from then on.

Where processing is based on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). To exercise these rights, contact the controller at the email address above.
